# Parallax artwork

Created with the built-in imagegen tool, using the existing stationery as edit references. Outputs resized and encoded to WebP with transparency preserved for the floral cutout.

Project assets:
- public/images/stationery-layered.webp
- public/images/stationery-layered-desktop.webp
- public/images/floral-corner-layer.webp

## mobilePaper

Precise-object-edit of supplied portrait wedding stationery. Remove ONLY the real blush pink rose bouquet, green leaves and small white fresh flowers from the UPPER LEFT corner. Seamlessly reconstruct the ivory paper and sculpted frame where that bouquet was; keep the upper-left corner as calm embossed ivory paper. Keep the ENTIRE lower-right rose bouquet, every other gold botanical detail, the exact scalloped arch/frame, paper texture, margins, original portrait composition and color palette unchanged. No text. No extra flowers. The upper-left fresh floral bouquet must be fully absent; this will be replaced by a separately animated floral layer. Maintain same 1:2 portrait aspect ratio.

## desktopPaper

Precise-object-edit of supplied landscape wedding stationery. Remove ONLY the fresh blush pink rose bouquet, green leaves and small white fresh flowers from the UPPER LEFT corner. Seamlessly reconstruct ivory paper and embossed botanical frame behind it. Keep the entire lower-right rose bouquet, exact ornate scalloped arch/frame, paper texture, gold vines, margins, lighting, original wide composition and colors unchanged. No text, no new decoration. The upper-left fresh bouquet must be fully absent: it will be replaced by a separately animated floral layer. Maintain same landscape 16:9 aspect ratio.

## floralLayer

Background-extraction asset edit. Extract ONLY the upper-left fresh blush rose bouquet from the reference wedding stationery, as a separate genuinely transparent PNG cutout. Preserve its natural pale peach/blush garden roses, a few green leaves, airy tiny white flowers, occasional delicate gold sprigs, realistic paper-like dimensional lighting and warm soft shadows. Remove ALL ivory paper, scalloped frame, gold border, embossed plaster decorations, background and the lower-right bouquet. Bouquet arranged as a corner piece, dense at the top-left, tapering gracefully toward the right and down, all isolated on alpha transparency. Keep realistic fine botanical edges; no white matte, no rectangle, no text. Landscape roughly 6:5 canvas, minimal transparent padding, no detached flecks.
